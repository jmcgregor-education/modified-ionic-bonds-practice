Ionic bonds: practice

Four short activities. Not graded.

Practice

Not graded. Try, get feedback, and try again until it clicks.

1. Finding the bond

Remember! An ionic bond is like a game of give and take. Metals are givers (shiny coins). Nonmetals are takers (clouds of gas). If you have one of each, they make a strong pull!

Look at each pair. Is there a giver and a taker?

2. Salt properties: match them

Tap a property on the left. Then tap what it means on the right.

3. Find the salt

Table salt is NaCl. Tap the picture that shows salt.

4. Cation or anion?

Look at the sign on each atom. Then pick its name.
